What to Expect

Live music, festivals and events

Hi, I’m Frank. I photograph the performance, the people who came for it, and the moments in between. I’m based in the South East and available for bookings across the UK.

Check Availability View Portfolio

01 · Enquiry

Tell me what you’re planning

Send me a few details about your gig, club night, festival or launch. If the date or venue is still up in the air, tell me what you know so far. We can work out what coverage makes sense for the event.

02 · The brief

We’ll agree what matters most

Maybe you need artist photos for press, crowd shots to promote the next night, or coverage that shows what your venue feels like when it’s full. We’ll talk through the running order, any must-have moments, and where the photos will be used.

03 · Coverage

On the night

I’ll move between the stage, the crowd and the quieter corners of the venue. I look for the moments that tell the story of the event, from the first people through the doors to the reaction when the room really gets going.

04 · Delivery

After the event

I’ll select and edit a set of photos that works together and gives you a mix of wide scenes, close-ups and candid moments. We’ll agree delivery timing before the event. If you need a few images for a next-day recap or a press deadline, let me know when we plan the coverage.
